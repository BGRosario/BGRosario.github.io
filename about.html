About Me

My name is Bryan Gonzalez Rosario, and I was born and raised in Ponce, Puerto Rico. Throughout my life, I struggled to notice the beauty of the world around me. However, when I started pursuing photography as a hobby, I slowly began to fall in love with it. My goal is to capture moments that evoke emotions and create images that set my work apart from others.

Throughout my creative journey, I hope to connect with people, discover new perspectives, and, most importantly, have fun along the way.
