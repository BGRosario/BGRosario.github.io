About Me

My name is Bryan Gonzalez Rosario, I was born and raised in Ponce, Puerto Rico. Througout my life, I had difficulties noticing the world around me, when I started persuing photography (as a hobby) I slowly began to fall in love with it. I want to create frames that generate emotions to people, that sets me apart from everyone else. During my creative journey, I wish to connect, discover, and most importantly, have fun.
